name: Release evidence boundaries
description: User's requirements for honest Me beta participation and launch decisions.
---

For Me, beta participation must be consented and test accounts clearly labelled.
Keep people living with dementia and family carers as distinct participant groups.
Native iOS/Android and actual OS lifecycle evidence cannot be replaced with a
narrow desktop browser or visibility events. Independent security review means
an independent human process, not the implementing agent or automated scanners.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires these checks before wider release and a
go/no-go report based on evidence rather than an automatic success label.

**How to apply:** Mark unavailable human, device, provider and operational evidence
as blocked/not run, retain accountable owners and disable incomplete safety-critical
features. Never infer participant consent, reviewer approval or staffing from fixtures.

Store preparation is distinct from store approval. For Me, do not submit or
release until the owner authorises the specific action. Me's 18+ eligibility
policy is distinct from a store-assigned age rating. Reviewer access must use
isolated synthetic accounts, never real-member identities or hardcoded credentials.

**Why:** The user explicitly required these store-release boundaries and accurate
SDK/processor disclosures, rather than an automatic submission or approval claim.

**How to apply:** Keep missing signing, account, real-device, review and operating
dependencies visible. Prepare drafts without treating them as approved policies;
recheck official current store requirements before upload.

The user is handling the technical iOS and Android builds separately. Keep the
current store-readiness assistance focused on policy, security and non-technical
requirements rather than building or signing the mobile apps.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to exclude technical builds from this work.

**How to apply:** Identify public policy URLs, operator/contact information,
declarations, legal review and actual safety operations. Do not treat this scope
change as approval to submit or release.
