name: Dementia brand colours
description: User's purple and yellow colour requirement for Me.
---

Use light purple for Me's social surfaces and yellow for the writing. The user
subsequently asked to make the purple light rather than dark. Use a deeper
golden-yellow shade on light surfaces for readable contrast, retaining bright
yellow on darker purple buttons.

**Why:** The user said these are the dementia colours.

**How to apply:** Preserve this palette across Me's pages, controls and branding
when making future visual changes, with readable contrast.
