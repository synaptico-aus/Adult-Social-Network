These directory regions do not imply authority to launch in every country.
Choose intended launch regions deliberately. The staff dashboard stores
region, area, pending/blocked/reviewed status, reviewer identity, date, reference
and review expiry. Entries are operator attestations, not independently verified
legal approval or certification. Missing/expired evidence stays blocked.
Never seed approved reviews or manufacture reviewer identities.

## Required evidence for each intended launch region

- **Privacy/legal:** qualified local reviewer and decision; data-controller roles,
  sensitive-data basis, purpose/consent, processors/subprocessors/regions, transfer
  safeguards, retention/erasure/backup exceptions, rights requests, breach duties,
  terms, accessibility and content-rights obligations; actual support channels.
- **Age assurance:** approved age scope, method/privacy tradeoffs, bypass handling,
  underage response, accessibility and escalation. Existing eligibility flags
  are not independent proof of age. Do not weaken sign-in or MFA to pass tests.
- **Sustainability:** responsible owner, actual observed hosting/storage/processing
  costs, inventory/egress, realistic staffed hours, urgent handoff, response gaps,
  budgets, funding assumptions, shutdown/closure and member data-return plan.
  Sponsorship is not assumed revenue. Any future sponsor must be disclosed;
  private health data may not be monetised and payments are not introduced.
- **Recovery:** actual retention/expiry from every provider, RPO/RTO, isolated
  restore/drill results, deletion/revocation replay and independent reviewer.
- **Rollback:** known compatible release/schema, write pause, backup, reconciliation
  of deleted data and revoked permissions, verification and rollback owner.
- **Staffing:** actual roster with time zones/holidays, verified on-call contacts,
  acknowledgement tests, measured first-human/welcome/resolution coverage, missed
  windows and escalation. No unsupported 24/7 claim.

Approval requires evidence reference, decision reason, qualified reviewer and
future review expiry, plus a real independent process outside this application's
form. Reassess on material product, provider, region or staffing changes.

## Other explicit release blockers

Operational malware screening/signature freshness, whole-video moderation
coverage, actual iOS/Android and OS lifecycle testing, assistive-technology account
testing, evidence/legal-hold policy enforcement, independently verified alert
delivery/dead-man monitoring, processor-erasure verification, tamper-evident audit
retention and reconciliation of actual costs remain subject to their separate
operational approvals. This phase does not approve video publishing.
