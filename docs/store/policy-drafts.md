# Policy drafting pack — NOT APPROVED, NOT EFFECTIVE

These drafts are for the owner and qualified launch-region privacy/legal and
safety reviewers. They are **not live policies, consent text, an approved legal
opinion or a reason to lift onboarding holds**. Missing legal identity,
contacts, jurisdictions, concrete retention periods and operating coverage
must be resolved, not replaced by invented values. Publish reviewed full
versions with version/effective dates, accessible links and archived versions.

## Privacy policy draft

Me provides adult social connection, messaging, groups, learning and shared
memories. The responsible operator and privacy contact must be identified
before release.

We process the account, profile, content, relationships, preferences, safety
reports and technical data needed to provide and protect the service. People
may include health information in profiles or content. Such information is
sensitive; Me is not a health-record service or treatment. The final notice
must match every flow in `data-disclosures.md`, including authentication,
hosting/storage, remote fonts, notification adapters and consented external
embeds. Explain purposes and lawful bases separately, processor locations,
international transfers and rights/contact procedures for each launch region.

People deliberately choose audiences. Collections and external Me shares do
not widen source permissions. External sites have their own practices;
tracking embeds require consent and a plain link remains available. Private
content must not go to translation providers without an established privacy
and consent basis. Me does not sell or monetise private health information.

Explain how to export data and request deletion, the identity verification
required, expected acknowledgement/completion periods and confirmation.
Identify precisely what is retained for a justified safety/legal reason, who
can access it, the review/expiry rules and actual backup/log expiry. Do not
promise instant removal from every backup or fixed expiry not verified with
providers. Explain limited offline revocation and logout/account-switch purge.
Optional service feedback does not include private text or health details.
Explain how to refuse/withdraw optional consent without losing core access.

**Still needed:** legal operator, privacy email, applicable laws and contacts,
processor contracts/locations, actual retention/deletion/backup schedule,
cookie/SDK/telemetry findings, final permissions, complaint/regulator routes
and independent approval of the exact version.

## Terms draft

Me is for people aged **18 and over**. This is Me's eligibility rule, separate
from a store-assigned age rating. Me offers social connection and education,
not emergency response, diagnosis, clinical assessment or treatment. If there
is immediate danger use appropriate local emergency services. State actual
moderation/support hours without claiming 24/7 coverage.

Keep accounts secure, use the reporting and blocking tools and respect other
people's privacy and chosen audiences. Do not impersonate others, harass,
exploit, threaten, scam or publish illegal material. Users must have rights
and consent for content they contribute. A collection or link does not grant
copyright or permission to rehost music/articles. Use permitted external links
or authorised embeds. Do not fabricate personal events or replacement memories.

Specify the narrow licence actually needed to host and deliver owned content
to its selected audiences, ownership, removal requests and a contactable
copyright process. Define proportionate sanctions, notice, appeals and lawful
exceptions. Explain account deletion and justified evidence retention by
reference to the reviewed privacy notice. Regional consumer rights,
liability, dispute handling and governing law require qualified review.

No payments are currently enabled. Any future paid digital feature requires
separate owner approval, current store-rule review and clear pricing,
cancellation/restoration terms. Disclose future sponsorship; never monetise
private health data.

**Still needed:** operator, support contact, regions, reviewed rights/licence,
appeal process/timeframes, governing law/consumer-rights clauses and approval.

## Community rules draft

- Be patient, respectful and clear. Do not shame anyone for memory, language,
  accessibility needs or asking for help.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate, scam or exploit others.
- Do not publish another person's private/health information without valid
  permission. Respect blocks and audience restrictions.
- No child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, sexualisation of children
  or CSAM. Use reporting tools; do not copy, forward or collect prohibited
  material as “proof”. Trained staff follow applicable reporting law.
- No illegal content, credible violence or dangerous misinformation. Do not
  present unreviewed health claims as professional advice or guaranteed cures.
- Post only material you may share. Music/articles need appropriate rights;
  links do not authorise downloading or rehosting.
- Report a post, message or member using in-app reporting. Block unwanted
  contact. Safety reports and appeals are reviewed within actual staffed
  coverage, not an invented response promise.
- Moderators can restrict or remove content and apply proportionate sanctions.
  Explain reasons and appeals except where law/safety prevents disclosure.
- This is not an emergency channel. Use local emergency help for immediate danger.

**Still needed:** public child-safety standards URL, trained child-safety
contact, actual staffed hours, urgent/illegal-content escalation,
jurisdiction-specific reporting, appeal timing and approved publication.
