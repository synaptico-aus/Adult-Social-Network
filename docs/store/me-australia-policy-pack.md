### 2.1 Who we are

Me is operated by Salveo Care PTY Ltd, based in Victoria, Australia.
Me supports adult social connection, shared experiences and educational
information for people affected by dementia. It is not an emergency service
or a substitute for individual professional health advice.

Privacy contact: `[TO COMPLETE: public privacy contact]`.  
Other legally required business/contact details:
`[TO COMPLETE: reviewed identification and contact information]`.

### 2.2 Information we handle

Depending on the enabled features you use, we handle:

- Account and authentication details, such as your chosen name, contact details,
  account identifiers, verification status and session/security information.
- Profile information and preferences you provide, including accessibility,
  language, selected broad region, time zone and notification choices.
- Posts, stories, comments, messages, photos, recordings, videos, captions,
  external links and Memory Collections you choose to create or share.
- Connections, circles, group memberships, blocks, saves, audience settings and
  interaction choices needed to provide the service and enforce permissions.
- Reports, appeals, consent and eligibility records, and proportionate evidence
  needed to investigate safety, security or legal concerns.
- Necessary device, network, service and diagnostic information, as identified
  in the final cookie, SDK, log and provider inventory.

Health-related fields and content may reveal sensitive health information
about you or someone else. Calling Me a social service does not make that
information non-sensitive.

We explain required and optional information at collection. Where consent is
required, we seek it before collecting, using or disclosing sensitive
information, unless a lawful exception applies. Only provide another person's
information where you have appropriate permission or other lawful authority.

Declining optional information or permissions may prevent a dependent feature
from working, but must not unnecessarily prevent access to unrelated features.
A dementia diagnosis does not automatically remove decision-making capacity.
A family or caring relationship alone does not authorise account access.

### 2.3 Purposes

We use information to provide requested features, apply permissions,
authenticate accounts, deliver selected notifications, support accessibility,
answer enquiries, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, investigate reports and
meet applicable legal obligations.

Optional service feedback and product measurement must not include private
text or health details. Account-linked measurement is not described as
anonymous merely because those details are excluded.

We do not sell or monetise private health information. Future sponsorship will
be clearly disclosed. Private content must not be sent to translation or other
AI providers without an established, disclosed privacy and consent basis.

### 2.4 Audiences and disclosures

Use the available audience and interaction controls to choose who may access
content and, where supported, react, comment or share. A collection does not
widen its source items' audiences. An external link to private Me content does
not grant access: authentication and original permissions still apply.

Authorised viewers may retain what they have already seen or capture a copy.
We cannot promise to recall every copy held on someone else's device.

Authorised staff may access information for a specific support, safety,
security or legal purpose under restricted access controls. Any disclosure
required or otherwise permitted by law must have an appropriate basis and be
limited to what is justified.

External sites have their own privacy practices. Tracking third-party embeds
require deliberate consent before loading, with a plain-link alternative.
Consent to an embed is not consent to disclose unrelated private content.
Machine translations, if enabled, must be labelled, retain their originals
and provide correction/reporting routes.
